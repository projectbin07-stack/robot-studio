Robot Studio

Open any robot model (URDF, xacro, a description package or a zip) or build one from a template, check it, see it in 3D and drive it in a simulated world, all in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Drive this robot in a 2D world with heights: simulated wheels, encoders, IMU and LiDAR at the robot's URDF frames, wheel odometry, scan matching and MCL. Everything here is model output, not a measurement.

Drive by hand at any time: W/S or arrows forward and back, A/D turn, Q/E strafe (mecanum), Space stop; a gamepad's sticks; or the on-screen sticks.

Edit the world

Drag to draw walls, boxes (a rectangle) and cylinders (centre to radius); click to erase or add a waypoint; drag from the start to set its heading. Obstacles lower than the LiDAR are drawn orange in 3D: the LiDAR cannot see them.
